    General
    
                
    
                    
                    
                
    
                
    
                    
                    
                
    
                
    
                    
    
                        
                        
                        
    
                            Enable and configure S3 in “Remote storage” to use remote backups.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                
    
                
    
                    
                    
                    
    Between fulls, each backup stores only what changed
                        since the previous one. Restore automatically combines the whole chain.
    
                
    
                
    
                    
                    
    When a chain is complete, keep only the restore
                        points your retention selects — each converted into a standalone Full dated
                        at its original time — and delete the rest of the chain. The current chain
                        keeps its daily Deltas until a new Baseline starts.
    
                
    

                
    Schedule
    
                
    
                    
                    
                
    
                
    
                    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    One or more runs per day — 24-hour format, server's timezone.
    
                
    
                
    
                    
                    
                    
    Runs from midnight at the chosen interval — server's timezone.
    
                
    

                
    Retention
    
                
    
                    
                    
                
    
                
    
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
                    
    
                    
    
                    
    Each row is an independent rule: it looks back over its
                        own window from today and keeps the newest "keep" backups per period. Rules
                        overlap — a backup survives if any rule keeps it; anything older than every
                        window is removed. Incremental chains count as one backup and are kept or
                        dropped as a whole.
    
                
    

                
    Exclusions
    
                
    
                    
                    
                    
    One per line, relative to the folder. Wildcards like *.log are supported.
